  <div class="method-band">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">Our method</p>
      <h2>Reliable automation includes the uncomfortable edge cases</h2>
    </div>
    <ol class="process-list process-list-dark">
      <li><strong>Map the actual work.</strong> Inputs, exceptions, approvals, and the person accountable for the result.</li>
      <li><strong>Build the smallest useful path.</strong> One measurable outcome before a broad transformation project.</li>
      <li><strong>Test bad inputs deliberately.</strong> Missing fields, duplicates, conflicting formats, outages, and plausible wrong answers.</li>
      <li><strong>Hand over evidence.</strong> Plain-language operating notes, review points, rollback, and ownership.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
