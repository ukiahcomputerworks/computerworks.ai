<p>A few tricks our engineers use every day on Windows, Mac, and the browser.</p>

<h2>Windows 10 &amp; 11</h2>
<ul class="directory-list">
  <li><strong>Win + L</strong>: Lock your workstation instantly before stepping away.</li>
  <li><strong>Win + Shift + S</strong>: Snip any part of your screen straight to the clipboard.</li>
  <li><strong>Win + Tab</strong>: Open Task View to see every window and virtual desktop at once.</li>
  <li><strong>Win + .</strong> (period): Open the emoji and symbol picker in any text field.</li>
  <li><strong>Win + V</strong>: Open clipboard history and paste something you copied earlier. Enable it first under Settings &gt; System &gt; Clipboard.</li>
  <li><strong>Ctrl + Shift + Esc</strong>: Jump straight to Task Manager, no right-click needed.</li>
  <li><strong>Win + Ctrl + Shift + B</strong>: Restart your graphics driver if the display freezes or glitches, without rebooting.</li>
</ul>

<h2>macOS</h2>
<ul class="directory-list">
  <li><strong>Cmd + Space</strong>: Spotlight search: launch an app, do quick math, or find a file.</li>
  <li><strong>Cmd + Shift + 4</strong>: Screenshot a selected area; add Space after to screenshot just one window.</li>
  <li><strong>Cmd + Ctrl + Q</strong>: Lock your screen instantly.</li>
  <li><strong>Three-finger drag</strong>: Enable it under Accessibility &gt; Pointer Control to move windows without holding the trackpad down.</li>
  <li><strong>Cmd + Option + Esc</strong>: Force-quit a frozen application.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)</h2>
<ul class="directory-list">
  <li><strong>Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + T</strong>: Reopen the last tab you closed by accident. Repeat to reopen more.</li>
  <li><strong>Ctrl/Cmd + L</strong>: Jump to the address bar from anywhere on the page.</li>
  <li><strong>Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + N</strong>: Open a private/incognito window.</li>
  <li><strong>Middle-click a link</strong>: Opens it in a new background tab without leaving your place.</li>
</ul>

<h2>General Security Habits</h2>
<ul class="directory-list">
  <li>Turn on automatic updates for your operating system and browser. Most real-world attacks exploit patches that were available months earlier.</li>
  <li>Use a password manager instead of reusing passwords; a breach on one site shouldn't compromise the rest.</li>
  <li>Enable two-factor authentication on email first. It's the account attackers use to reset everything else.</li>
  <li>Back up important files to at least two places: one local (external drive) and one offsite (cloud).</li>
</ul>
