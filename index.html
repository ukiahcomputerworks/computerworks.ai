AI Integration & Workflow Automation

More of our clients are asking how to put AI tools to work without creating a bigger mess than they started with — and that's become part of what we do. We can help with:

  • AI integration — connecting AI assistants and large language models (LLMs) to the tools you already use: email, documents, scheduling, and internal systems.
  • Prompt engineering — building reliable, reusable prompts so your team gets consistent results instead of trial and error.
  • Workflow automation — automating repetitive tasks between apps so your team spends less time on busywork and more time on the job.
  • Staff training — walking your team through how to use these tools well, and where the guardrails should be.
  • Data privacy review — making sure any AI tool you adopt handles your business and customer data responsibly.

No hype, no pressure to adopt anything before you're ready — just honest advice on whether AI is actually a good fit for a given task, and hands-on help getting it set up when it is.
